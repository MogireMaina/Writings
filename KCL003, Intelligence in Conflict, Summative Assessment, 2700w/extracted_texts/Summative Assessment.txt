Summative Assessment  
Deadline: 17 December 2025  
Writing Style: Chicago 17 Manual and Conventions  
The summative assessment for this module is  a 2,700 to 3,000 -word essay , (includes 
footnotes and essay only)  
 
The task  
You must select  one  of the following questions for your essay:  
• Q1: Evaluate the use of intelligence in conventional war in comparison with its use 
in irregular war.  
• Q2: To what extent do modern military commanders successfully balance 
intelligence and intuition? Consider at least two case studies in your answer.  
• Q3: To what degree might different intelligence sources impact decision -making in 
war? Discuss with reference to at least two intelligence sources.

Construal and Ideology
•Ideology  = “systematically organised presentation of 
reality”  (Hodge & Kress 1993: 15) usually in the interests of 
existing power structures
•Made possible in language through construal – “our 
manifest ability to conceive and portray the same situation 
in alternate ways”  (Langacker 2008: 43)
•Dimensions of construal: image schemas, viewpoint, 
attention, metaphor
•Cognitive CDA (e.g. Hart 2024) considers ideological functions 
of construal operations in political and media communication

Metaphor
•Metaphor and framing:
14"Tax relief" imposes the additional metaphor that Taxation Is an 
Affliction, with the entailments that the president is a hero for 
attempting to remove this affliction and the Democrats are bad 
guys for opposing him …. There is of course another way to 
think about taxes: Taxes are membership fees used to 
maintain and expand services and the infrastructure. But 
however true this may be, it is not yet an established frame 
inscribed in the synapses of our brains. (Lakoff 2004)
Metaphor has consequences for how a particular issue is ‘framed’ or 
structured, which aspects are foregrounded and which are 
backgrounded, what inferences are facilitated, what evaluative and 
emotional associations triggered, what courses of action seem to be 
possible and so on. (Semino 2008: 91)Political and economic ideologies are framed in metaphorical terms. 
Like all other metaphors, political and economic metaphors can hide 
aspects of reality. But in the area of politics and economics, 
metaphors matter more, because they constrain our lives. (Lakoff & 
Johnson 1980: 236)

Metaphor: Wars and Wildfires
•Covid -19 framed as WAR by governments around world 
(Filardo -Llamas 2021)
•War metaphors convey gravity of situation and 
encourage collective action / compliance (Flusberg et al. 
2018)
•At same time, potentially problematic in increasing 
fatalism, contradicting message to stay at home, failing 
to accurately represent contagion, and falsely implying 
victory is possible (Hanne 2022; Semino 2021)
•PANDEMIC  IS WILDFIRE  metaphor may be more apt and 
more effective strategy in public health crisis 
communication (Semino 2021)

Metaphor: Wars and Wildfires
•War metaphors more effective than fire

intention to adopt pro -health behaviours,
